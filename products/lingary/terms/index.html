Lingary is an independent project by an individual developer, referred to below as "we". There is no company, no backend and no user account. How your data is handled is described separately in the privacy policy.

2. Bring your own key

In online mode Lingary talks to an AI provider you choose — OpenAI, Google, Groq, Fish Audio or your own endpoint — using an API key you obtained yourself. We do not resell, proxy or subsidise access to any provider.

That means: you are responsible for keeping your key confidential, for whatever the provider charges you, and for complying with that provider's own terms. If a provider suspends your account or changes its pricing, that is between you and them.

4. Acceptable use

Beyond the restrictions above, don't use Lingary to break the law, to harass anyone, or to attack the app or the services it talks to. Don't try to pass synthesised speech off as a real person's voice.

The app runs on your device and its source is not public, but you are welcome to inspect its behaviour. Reverse engineering is limited only to the extent your local law permits it.

5. What the AI says is not advice

Lingary is a practice partner, not a teacher, examiner or certifying body. Language models get things wrong: grammar explanations can be incorrect, corrections can be wrong, a CEFR level the app estimates is an estimate and nothing more. Nothing the app produces is medical, legal, financial or professional advice.

Check anything that matters against a real source before relying on it.
