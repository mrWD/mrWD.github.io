GoalsTable — privacy policy

GoalsTable has no servers and no accounts. Your goals stay on your device, and the app makes no network requests at all.

What this covers

This policy is about the GoalsTable app — the iOS build distributed through TestFlight, the Android build, and the web version. The site you are reading it on is covered separately by Legal & privacy.

GoalsTable is an independent project. There is no company backend, no sign-up and no user account. “We” below means the developer of the app.

What we store

Nothing. Your goals, your monthly plans and your daily check-ins live in your browser’s storage on the web, and in the app’s own private storage in the installed app. Deleting your browsing data or the app deletes them — which is why the app keeps offering you an export.

What leaves your device

Nothing. GoalsTable queries no catalogue and has no backend of its own: everything it shows, it holds locally. The app works with the network switched off and behaves the same way when it is on.

Analytics

None, in the installed app and on the web alike. No page views, no events, no identifiers, and no crash reporting of our own.

Backups

An exported backup — JSON or CSV — is an ordinary file created at your request. It goes wherever you send it: your Files, your drive, your email. Never to us.

Beta testing

While the app is distributed through TestFlight, Apple collects the installation and crash data described in Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice. That is Apple’s collection under Apple’s terms. It reaches us only as anonymous crash reports, and never includes anything you typed into the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Since the app collects nothing, any change would be about something newly added — and it would be described here before it shipped.
