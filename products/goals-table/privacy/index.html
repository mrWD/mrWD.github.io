GoalsTable — privacy policy

GoalsTable has no accounts and no sync. Your goals stay on your device. The one request the app makes is an anonymous usage count, once a day, and you can turn it off.

What this covers

This policy is about the GoalsTable app — the iOS build distributed through TestFlight, the Android build, and the web version. The site you are reading it on is covered separately by Legal & privacy.

GoalsTable is an independent project. There is no company backend, no sign-up and no user account. “We” below means the developer of the app.

What we store

None of your goals. Your goals, your monthly plans and your daily check-ins live in your browser’s storage on the web, and in the app’s own private storage in the installed app. Deleting your browsing data or the app deletes them — which is why the app keeps offering you an export. What we do keep are the running totals of the usage statistics below, which hold nothing per device.

What leaves your device

Your goals never do. GoalsTable queries no catalogue and keeps nothing of yours on a server: everything it shows, it holds locally. The only request it makes is the daily usage count described in the next section, and only while that is switched on. The app works with the network switched off and behaves the same way when it is on.

Usage statistics

To see how many people use GoalsTable and which parts are worth more work, the app keeps a few counters on your device and sends them in one request a day, the first time you open it that day. It is on unless you turn it off; the app says so in one line the first time you open it. Versions without the switch described below send nothing. The request carries exactly this:

  • the app’s name, the platform (iOS, Android or web) and the app version;
  • the language the app is shown in;
  • your device’s date that day — the date, not the time;
  • the week you started using a version with statistics, or “earlier” if you used GoalsTable before they existed;
  • whether this is the first request ever, the first of the week and the first of the month;
  • how many times each of a fixed set of actions happened since the last request — a goal ticked off, a backup exported, a screen opened;
  • how many errors of each kind happened, by type alone, such as TypeError.

It never carries an identifier of any kind — no device, user, advertising or session ID — and none of your goals, plans, check-ins or anything else you type, no error messages and no location. Nothing in it says at what time anything happened.

The request goes to a small function of ours on Vercel, which adds the counts to running totals in an Upstash Redis database: per app, per day, week and month, nothing per device or per request. The totals are deleted after 400 days. The function sees your IP address, as any server does, and never stores or logs it; to stop one address flooding it, it holds a scrambled, one-way form of the address in memory for ten minutes. Vercel keeps a log of the requests it serves, your IP address among them, for about an hour, under Vercel’s privacy policy.

The numbers are not used for advertising or to track you, not combined with any other data, and not shared or sold. There is no crash reporting of our own beyond these counts.

Turning it off: Profile → Share anonymous usage statistics. Switching it off stops the requests and deletes the counters kept on your device. In a browser that sends Global Privacy Control, the web version starts with it off.

Backups

An exported backup — JSON or CSV — is an ordinary file created at your request. It goes wherever you send it: your Files, your drive, your email. Never to us.

Beta testing

While the app is distributed through TestFlight, Apple collects the installation and crash data described in Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice. That is Apple’s collection under Apple’s terms. It reaches us only as anonymous crash reports, and never includes anything you typed into the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, of any age: the usage counts above carry no identifiers and nothing you type.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it, and anything newly sent anywhere is described here before it ships.
