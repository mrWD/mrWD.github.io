Double Subtitles

Watch with subtitles in your native and your target language at the same time. When a line teaches you a word, save it straight to Anki or Quizlet without leaving the episode.

  • Free
  • Open source
  • No data collected
Add to Chrome Source on GitHub

What it looks like

What it does

  • 📺

    Two subtitle tracks at once

    The language you're learning and the one you already know, stacked over the player — instead of switching tracks back and forth.

  • 🔤

    Word by word

    Open the current line and it breaks apart into its words with a translation for each, so you can see which one you actually missed.

  • 🕘

    A searchable history

    Every line you've passed stays in a sidebar you can scroll and search, which makes going back to “that phrase from earlier” trivial.

  • 🗂️

    Saves into your study tools

    Send a word or a whole sentence to Anki or Quizlet — the card comes pre-filled — or keep it in the browser, or append it to a Google Sheet of your own.

  • 🎨

    Styling you control

    Subtitle appearance and position are adjustable, so the second line doesn't fight with the player's own controls.

  • 🔒

    Nothing is collected

    No accounts and no analytics. What you watch stays between you and the streaming service.

Where it works

  • Netflix
  • Amazon Prime Video
  • Disney+
  • YouTube
  • Twitch

Prime Video is covered on primevideo.com and on the Amazon storefronts — .com, .co.uk, .de and .co.jp.

Saved words land in Anki (AnkiWeb), Quizlet, the browser itself, or a Google Sheet you point it at.

Installing it

From the store

Install it from the Chrome Web Store — this is the easy path.

From source

Clone the repository, open your browser's extensions page, turn on Developer mode and use Load unpacked.

Then open a video on a supported site, click the extension icon and pick your two languages in the popup.

What's planned

Apple TV support; Firefox, Opera and Safari builds; showing the base form of verbs and nouns alongside the subtitle; binding subtitles to the video timeline for tighter sync; and exporting the whole history, split by title.
