Double Subtitles

Watch with subtitles in your native and your target language at the same time. When a line teaches you a word, save it straight to Anki or Quizlet without leaving the episode.

  • Free
  • Open source
  • No data collected
Add to Chrome Source on GitHub

The idea

One line is the language you're learning, the other is the one you already know. You stop pausing to translate, and you stop guessing whether you got it right.

What it does

  • 📺

    Two subtitle tracks at once

    Both languages render together over the player, instead of you switching tracks back and forth.

  • 🎨

    Styling you control

    Subtitle appearance and position are adjustable, so the second line doesn't fight with the player's own controls.

  • 🗂️

    Words go to Anki and Quizlet

    Save a word or a whole sentence while you watch and build a deck out of what you actually met on screen.

  • 🔒

    Nothing is collected

    No accounts, no analytics. What you watch stays between you and the streaming service.

Where it works

  • Netflix
  • Amazon Prime Video
  • Disney+

Learning platforms it saves to: Anki and Quizlet.

Installing it

From the store

Install it from the Chrome Web Store — this is the easy path.

From source

Clone the repository, open your browser's extensions page, turn on Developer mode and use Load unpacked.

Then open a video on a supported site, click the extension icon and pick your two languages in the popup.

What's planned

Apple TV support; Firefox, Opera and Safari builds; showing the base form of verbs and nouns alongside the subtitle; binding subtitles to the video timeline for tighter sync; and exporting the whole history, split by title.
