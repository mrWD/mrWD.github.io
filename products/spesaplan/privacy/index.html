SpesaPlan — privacy policy

SpesaPlan has no accounts of its own and no analytics. Your plans, pantry and prices stay on your phone, and receipt photos are read there too. The few requests it makes go to open public services, and only when you use the feature that needs them.

What this covers

This policy is about the SpesaPlan app — the iOS build distributed through TestFlight, the Android build, and the web version. The site you are reading it on is covered separately by Legal & privacy.

SpesaPlan is an independent project. There is no company backend and no sign-up. “We” below means the developer of the app.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter: the budget, the number of people, diet and allergies, the shops you picked, the weekly plan, the pantry and its use-by dates, and the prices you typed or scanned. If you set a goal, also your height, weight, age and activity level, which are used only to work out calorie and protein targets on the phone. All of it lives in the app’s own storage; deleting the app deletes it. We do not have a copy.

What leaves your device

Only this, and only when you use the feature:

  • 📍

    OpenStreetMap

    When you choose a shop, the name of the city in your profile goes to the public Overpass API (overpass-api.de, overpass.private.coffee or overpass.kumi.systems) to list the supermarkets there. The answer is kept on the phone for a month.

  • 🏷️

    Open Food Facts

    When you scan or type a barcode, that number goes to world.openfoodfacts.org to look the product up, and its photo is loaded from there. Nothing else is sent with it.

  • 🔄

    Your own server, if any

    Sync between devices and live shop prices need a SpesaPlan server whose address you enter yourself. Sync sends your profile, plan, pantry and prices to that server under a random code, and the server keeps only a hash of the code. The beta has no server set, so by default none of this happens.

Receipt photos and pantry photos are never uploaded: the text on a receipt is recognised on the phone itself, by Apple’s Vision framework on iOS and Google’s ML Kit on Android. The official price survey the app uses ships inside the app, so reading it sends nothing.

Permissions

The camera is used to scan barcodes and photograph receipts; photo library access, to pick a receipt or a pantry photo you already took. Local network access is asked for only to reach a SpesaPlan server on your own Wi-Fi. Each is asked for when you first use the feature, and the app works without any of them.

Analytics

None. No page views, no events, no identifiers, no advertising and no crash reporting of our own.

Beta testing

While the app is distributed through TestFlight, Apple collects the installation and crash data described in Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice. That is Apple’s collection under Apple’s terms. It reaches us only as anonymous crash reports and the feedback you choose to send, and never includes your plans, pantry or prices.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it, and anything new the app sends anywhere is described here before it ships.
