Daylight — terms of use

Daylight is a one-person project with no account and no subscription. These terms say what you can expect from it, and above all what it is not: it is not a doctor.

Not medical advice

Daylight is a wellness and training aid. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and it is not a substitute for a doctor, a physiotherapist or a qualified coach. Its messages are general information built from your own recent data.

When the app suggests you discuss something with a doctor, take it as a prompt to ask, not as a finding. When it says nothing, that does not mean nothing is wrong.

In an emergency — chest pain or pressure, difficulty breathing, fainting, sudden weakness or numbness, a sudden loss of hearing, or anything that frightens you — call your local emergency number. Do not wait for, or rely on, the app.

Training and safety

You decide what you do. The app never recommends a working weight: where a jump looks too large it asks you to check the entry, and that is all. Stop if something hurts, and talk to a professional before starting or changing a programme, especially if you are ill, injured, pregnant, taking medication or have a heart or circulation condition. You use the training suggestions at your own risk.

How good the numbers are

Figures come from Apple Health and from what you enter. A watch measures many things as estimates, a missing or noisy history can mislead, and a figure from a third-party app or typed in by hand is given less weight. Each conclusion carries a confidence level; a single doubtful reading is never treated as a serious finding. Blood-pressure and temperature readings are only as good as the device that took them.

The on-device language model

The model only rewords an answer the rules have already produced. Its text is checked and discarded if it names a weight or an illness, but a model can still be clumsy or wrong; the “Why?” screen shows the facts the answer rests on. It needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence and is not available everywhere; without it the app shows the rule-based wording.

Daylight is free, for your personal, non-commercial use. Do not use it to monitor another person without their knowledge, and do not rely on it for any decision where a mistake could hurt someone.
