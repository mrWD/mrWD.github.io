IT Skills — privacy policy

IT Skills has no servers and no accounts. Your progress stays on your device. The app sends something over the network only when you ask it to publish a project to your own GitHub account.

What this covers

This policy is about the IT Skills app, listed on the App Store as “IT Skills: Senior Path” — the iOS build distributed through TestFlight, the Android build, and the web version. The site you are reading it on is covered separately by Legal & privacy.

IT Skills is an independent project. There is no company backend, no sign-up and no user account. “We” below means the developer of the app.

What we store

Nothing. Everything the app keeps lives on your device: the name you typed in, your self-assessment, your skill levels, lesson and test results, the files of your course projects, certificates and interview scores. In the installed app that is the app’s own private storage; on the web it is your browser’s storage. Deleting the app or your browsing data deletes it.

If you give the app a GitHub personal access token, it is kept apart from everything else: in the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore, and on the web only in the memory of the open tab, gone when you close it. It is never written to plain storage and never included in a backup.

What leaves your device

Only what you publish. When you press Publish to GitHub on a finished course project and have saved a token, the app sends that project’s files and the repository name to api.github.com, authenticated with your token, to create the repository in your account. That request goes from your device straight to GitHub — never through us — and GitHub’s own privacy policy applies to what it receives. Without a token, publishing is simulated on the device and nothing is sent.

Nothing else goes out. Lessons, exercises and the course catalogue ship inside the app; your code runs on the device; interview answers are scored on the device by matching key terms, not by an online AI service. Links you tap open in your browser, and sharing a certificate or copying a backup happen only when you choose to.

Analytics

None, in the installed app and on the web alike. No page views, no events, no identifiers, and no crash reporting of our own.

Backups and deleting your data

Профиль (Profile) → Скопировать копию puts your progress on the clipboard as text, at your request, so you can keep it wherever you like and restore it on another device. It goes wherever you paste it. Never to us. The GitHub token is not part of it.

Профиль → Сбросить прогресс (reset progress) deletes everything the app stores, the saved token included. Deleting the app does the same.

Beta testing

While the app is distributed through TestFlight, Apple collects the installation and crash data described in Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice. That is Apple’s collection under Apple’s terms. It reaches us only as anonymous crash reports and the feedback you choose to send, and never includes your progress or anything you typed into the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Since the app collects nothing, any change would be about something newly added — and it would be described here before it shipped.
