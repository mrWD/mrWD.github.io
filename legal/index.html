Privacy

This website collects nothing about you. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts. The only thing stored is your light/dark theme preference, kept in your browser's own local storage so the page doesn't flash the wrong colour on your next visit. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

The site is served as static files by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes connection data such as your IP address in order to deliver the page; that's covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

The projects themselves are separate programs with their own behaviour. The trackers keep your library on your device. The browser extensions are additionally covered by the privacy disclosures in their store listings. Where a project sends anything to a third party, its own page and repository say so.
